📖 THE BOOK OF BORTSCH — EXPANDED EDITION

In the beginning, there was the Beet. The universe was without form, and void — a dark and tasteless expanse. Then the First Babushka reached across the formless deep, took the Beet in her ancient hands, and said: "It needs more dill."

From that primordial act of seasoning, all of creation emerged. The planets formed as giant beets. Stars are just very hot cauldrons. The Big Bang was a pressure cooker reaching critical mass. Scientists know this. They simply do not discuss it at conferences.

The Bortsch Clicker Expanded Edition introduces the Grand Stew system — the discovery that combining ingredients from across the cosmos produces meta-bortsch, a substance that bends the very rules of bowl-physics. The first alchemists tried to turn lead into gold; they were attempting the wrong transmutation. The correct target was bortsch.

The Prestige Tree, known in ancient texts as the Spoon of Heaven, allows those who have transcended mere mortal bortsch production to invest their divine currency into permanent cosmic upgrades. Five branches spread from the trunk: The Ladle Path (clicking), The Cauldron Path (buildings), The Dill Path (global), The Beet Path (prestige), and The Void Path (secret).

The Stock Exchange was founded in 1887 by Baron Vladimir Bortschemanoff, who realized that speculative investment in beet commodities was more reliable than actual beets. He was correct. The Baron is now a spirit who haunts the exchange, whispering "buy low" in the ears of traders.

— Inscribed on the Eternal Ladle, 4th Age of Bortsch
