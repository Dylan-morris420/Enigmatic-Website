Shaggy scanned the room for his tool, and he saw a smudge of red in the shadows, sitting in between two crates, up against the wall. Shaggy creeped towards it cautiously, and ran to the exit. Half way up the ladder, Shaggy realized that if he went back up he'd risk the monster seeing him through a window. So Shaggy stayed in the creepy basement. Eventually he calmed himself down. He rolled himself a joint with his emergency weed supply hidden in a vent. He got hungry, so he opened up the freezer to grab some Eggo's.

Scooby and I would always pull all nighters in this basement, and survive on eggo's. 

Shaggy looked over behind a crate in the corner of the room, Scooby hid there once to scare him. Shaggy's triple stacked waffle with Doritos and pickles ended up on Scooby's face. They laughed until they fell asleep. Curled up in a ball together on the dirty floor.

Shaggy licked his fingers after the waffle was in his belly, and took the crowbar with him as he peeked from under the trapdoor. The coast was clear.

I have to go find Scooby, I know he's out there.

Shaggy stuffed some cheez-its in his pocket, and bravely unlocked the door, staring into the complete and utter darkness. he exhaled and took the first step, he marched out onto the cool desert sand, and listened to all the bugs chirping, the street lamps humming. He heard a dog barking in the distance.

He looked around at all the houses, one house still had lights on. Some left their TV's on, and could be seen through the window.

He decided that he would check all of their sandwich spot's first, as those were the closest.

Then their smoke spots.

Then the pina colada spots. Those were the furthest.

after checking pina colada spot #2, Shaggy sat down on a nearby bench. His crowbar fell out of his hands into the sand. Shaggy shifted to lay on his side, and the cheez its were pulverized into crumbs instantly. Shaggy ate em' anyway.
