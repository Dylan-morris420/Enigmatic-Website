The Enigma Classification
by Dylan & Cody Morris
Act One

"So I walk into the place, right?"

Scooby is already chuckling.

"Hold on, hold on, wait for it. So get this, the guy wanted 50 dollars for his "Groovy Dooby Doobies", and I was like 'cincuenta dólares?? Hell nah man'"

Scooby absolutely LOSES his shit, curled up on the floor wheezing. Shaggy chuckles nervously. Shaggy takes a hit off the bong, he holds it.... holds it... then elegantly puffs the smoke into a giant mushroom cloud.

He passes the bong to Scooby, who's still laughing, and he inhales for about 5 seconds then immediately cracks up again, coughing simultaneously.

A giant pillar of smoke drifts out of the sunroof into the night air. Bob Marley hums low through the van's speakers.

Inside his tent, Fred stares at the ceiling. Listening. Waiting, like maybe this is the moment they finally stop laughing at the SAME joke, retold.

Scooby wheezes again.

Fred closes his eyes.

"God..."

They've been doing this all night long...

"They're STILL up?"

He sits up.

"WOULD YOU GUYS GO TO SLEEP ALREADY?"

"Oh shit — Scoob. Act natural."

"RAGGY. It's the rolice. It's the ROLICE—"

"Nahhh Scooby it's Fred"

"SAME THING!!!!"

"You guys do realize we have to be up by six o'clock, right!?"

"Like scoob, we should totally sneak out dude"

"Reah! Reah!"

Daphne groaned and pulled her pillow over her head.

Across from her, Velma lets out a long, exaggerated sigh, clearly pissed the fuck off.

The dying campfire lets out a tiny crackle. Crickets. The low sound of a stream somewhere nearby.

And something else... Like a tree swaying in the wind.

But the only wind tonight is from Scooby's lungs, and he's somehow STILL laughing his ass off over Shaggy's half-baked joke.

Once the laughing died out, Scooby and Shaggy fled the scene.

*Click* Shaggy turned on his flashlight. It flickered; the battery hummed. It was a professional-grade flashlight made specifically for mystery solving. It was not used for mystery solving. It was used for times when Scooby and Shaggy get so high out of their minds they decide to explore a creepy forest together.

Shaggy points his flashlight at a patch of moss on the tree in front of him. A slight rustle of the bushes behind him. Shaggy turns around to investigate but is interrupted as Scooby exclaims from behind another bush somewhere,

"Raggy, a clue, a clue!"

Shaggy set his flashlight down on a tree stump, and marched over to Scooby.

"Like, what'dya find Scoob?"

Scooby turns to Shaggy and his gaze widens. He goes silent.

"What is it pal?"

Scooby stammers,

"R-R-R-R-R-Raggy..... R-R-Ronster!!"

"What monster Scoob? I don't see a monster."

A shadow looms over Shaggy behind him, cast by the dim flashlight. Lightning strikes. It starts raining. Shaggy sees the giant figure towering over him. Shaggy shudders, then yelps—

"ZOINKS!"

Scooby jumps into Shaggy's arms and they bolt it to the Mystery Machine. They can hardly see in the pitch black darkness. Somehow their path led them straight back to the monster. Shaggy's feet skid against the wet dirt. Lightning again. The monster has glowing red eyes, a crooked twig nose, and a sinister smile, laughing at their fear.

Shaggy and Scooby split up, running in different directions. Shaggy runs behind a tree. The monster follows. Scooby comes out of a bush and runs straight into Shaggy, and into his arms.

At this point, Fred and the gang were fed up with whatever the hell these two were doing. So they get up and go investigate. They find a broken flashlight on a tree stump. Velma picks it up,

"These idiots! Do they know how much a replacement would be? This lighting apparatus was made SPECIFICALLY for mystery solving! Its professional grade! The premium package! They can't just—"

"Velma shut up for one minute."

Fred groaned.

"Look, we're all tired. Let's just go find them so we can go to bed."

"Freddie, its 2:00 in the morning, we wont get any sleep anyways"

"*sigh* You're right, well, you know the drill. Lets split up, Daphne — you come with—"

"I go with you, Velma goes alone, and Scooby and Shaggy just be themselves. Yeah. We know Fred."

Fred gasped, clutching his chest.

"Well o-kay then, if you dont want to go with me so bad, fine! hmph, I didn't want to go with you anyways. Matter of fact — Velma, you're comin' with me."

"Are you kidding me right now? Is your ego that fragile!?"

"Daph, you have no idea how much I do for you, I think you should show me a bit more respect!"

"Fred, you're an egocentric, narcissistic jerk! I'm going with Daphne for a change."

Velma and Daphne storm off into the woods.

"And don't you forget! You still have to play bait Daphne!"

Fred yelled after them, since he just NEEDED to get the last word in.

"Stupid Daphne... Stupid nerdy Velma always taking her side. Pfft, whatever. Fred's got this. Fred solo. Goin' commando."

He muttered to himself.

Shaggy adjusted his footing on the tree. Fred jumped at the noise.

"Like, Fred, I don't think that means what you think it means."

Shaggy and Scooby chuckled.

"Wha—? Guys!?! What the hell are you doing all the way up there?"

"A monster chased us up here! You should have seen it Fred, it was big, scary, and, and—"

"Yeah yeah, sure. What I think is that you two are on a different planet right now."

"And it had a huge rhenis!"

"What..? No, Scooby—"

"That was a joke, rehehehe!"

"Oh man, you really got me good Scooby!"

"ENOUGH. You two have obviously had way too much of those... What do you call them? Doobie snacks? And now you're seeing things. There's no monster. There's never a monster. Well — there's always a monster, but not THAT kind of monster. Now get down from there, both of you."

"Actually Fred, it's a common misconception that weed causes hallucinations, according to—"

"I DONT care Shaggy. Whatever you saw, it wasn't real."

✦ ✦ ✦
Meanwhile —

The girls search around a nearby cave.

"Do you really think they could be in there, Daphne?"

"Scooby and Shaggy would know better than to step foot in that creepy cave, no matter how high they are they're still chickens!"

"Yeah, but chickens dont keep you up all night — they just roost up in a tree or something."

"Hold on Velma, you might be on to something! What if they've climbed a tree or something?"

"I just said that, Daphne."

"Hold on, do you hear that?"

Fred was yelling at Scooby and Shaggy in the distance.

"I DONT CARE IF THERE'S A MONSTER, GET DOWN HERE — NOW!!! HOW DID YOU EVEN GET UP THERE? HOW HIGH ARE YOU!?"

"No Fred, it's 'Hi, how are you?' 'I'm fine, how are you?'"

Daphne turns to look at Velma, but to her surprise she had vanished.

"Velma? Where'd you go, hey! This isn't funny Velma where are you?"

She turns around, and is met face to face with the monster. She screams. She runs.

This gets the guys' attention.

"Daphne..? Oh god! Daphne, I have to go save her!"

Fred runs off into the darkness, leaving Scooby and Shaggy still up in the tree. Fred — completely blind in the dark — stumbles into his own trap. He's immediately tangled up in a net, twenty-something feet up in the air, screaming for help.

"SCOOBY, SHAGGY, velma.... i guess.... SOMEONE! HELP ME!"

The monster hears this commotion and charges toward Fred's direction — stepping on the shattered glass of the professional-grade lighting apparatus. It yelps in pain and trips over a rock onto a tree stump.

*Thump*

Just like that, the scary monster was taken out.

"Ugh, my hair is ruined. I've got like 80% sticks and twigs and 20% dirt and rocks. This is the worst night EVER!"

"Daphne, we have bigger things to worry about. Do you not see how structurally unsound this cage is? Jinkies, even the three little piggies could blow this thing down."

"Blow it down then, piggy."

Velma slowly turns around.

"What did you just call me? Get over here you bitch!"

Velma lunges at Daphne, taking the cage down with her. But their tussle is interrupted by the monster letting out a hurt moan and shuffling a bit. They both stop and look up at the monster, the broken flashlight flickering brighter than it probably should.

"Shit — tie it up!"

They both panic and reluctantly help each other tie the monster up. The flashlight has now stopped flickering and has caught fire to the tree monster's branches. Now they are both screaming, tying it up and putting the fire out simultaneously.

Fred is still screaming in the distance, his voice getting hoarse.

"Why... Why does this happen to me? Tell me trap, what have I done to deserve this?!! I brought you into this world and I can take you out of it! Take this!"

Fred does.... something... He's flailing his limbs about, trying to escape.
